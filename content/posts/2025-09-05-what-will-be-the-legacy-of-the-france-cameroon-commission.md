Macron announced the [creation of the commission](https://www.justiceinfo.net/en/119945-colonial-past-historians-commission-reconcile-cameroun.html) in July 2022, at a joint press conference with Cameroon’s long-time President Paul Biya, a move seen as part of [Macron’s promises to deal with France’s colonial past](https://www.justiceinfo.net/en/115285-colonial-crimes-reparations-movement-stalls-europe.html). The commission was co-chaired by [French historian Karine Ramondy and Cameroonian musician Blick Bassy](https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20230217-guerre-du-cameroun-blick-bassy-et-karine-ramondy-codirigeront-une-commission-m%C3%A9moire), who dedicated one of his albums, “1958”, to assassinated anti-colonialist leader [Ruben Um Nyobè](https://www.justiceinfo.net/en/82062-cameroon-um-nyobe-hero-symbol-french-colonial-crimes.html). Bassy was in charge of the cultural aspect of the commission’s work. The “research” commission was composed of seven French and seven Cameroonian historians, of whom five are women and nine men.
