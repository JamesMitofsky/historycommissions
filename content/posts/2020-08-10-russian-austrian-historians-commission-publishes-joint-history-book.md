On January 16, 2020, Russian-Austrian Joint Historians' Commission (JHC) publishes Vol. 2 of a joint textbook «Россия – Австрия: вехи совместной истории» [Russia - Austria: Milestones of Shared History]. You may watch a live presentation at the Gaidar Forum [here](https://youtu.be/-mU09X4Ig8k).
