The Polish-Ukrainian Congress of Historians met in Baranuv-Sandomyrsky over three days, nine problematic-chronological panels with the participation of over a hundred historians of both countries inspired and fruitfully discussed-discussed-shared achievements, experience and plans for the future.This event has become iconic in all respects at least because the joint Polish-Ukrainian historical forums have not convened for a long time, and similar to this year's meeting of researchers of ALL eras of the joint history of Ukraine and Poland, have not been held at all during the times of independence.
