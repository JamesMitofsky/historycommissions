On January 15, 2016 Ukrainian Catholic University hosted the session of German-Ukrainian Commission of Historians. During
the session historians discussed the work plan of the Commission for
the period 2016 - 2018, defined the main directions of its activities
including its main vector of assisting young researchers of History of
Ukrainian-German relations and Holocaust. Historians negotiated
regarding the organization of annual summer school, which will take
place in Germany and Ukraine alternatively. They also discussed the
possibility of exchanging students and young scholars between
universities and scholarly institutions of the two countries. For further information, read [here](http://tkuma.dp.ua/index.php/en/the-latest-news/news/873-zasidannya-ukrajinsko-nimetskoji-istorichnoji-komisiji-2).
