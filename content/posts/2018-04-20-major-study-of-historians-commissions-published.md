The first major study of historians' commissions in Europe--both internal and international commissions--has been published by the German publisher DE GRUYTER OLDENBOURG. Entitled *Historikerkommissionen und historische Konfliktbewältigung* [Historical Commissions and Managing Conflict Over History] it is by Christoph Cornelißen and Paolo Pezzino. Click [here](https://www.degruyter.com/view/product/487457) for the Table of Contents and Introduction.
