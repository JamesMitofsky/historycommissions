On March 23, the Japan-Korea Joint History Study (Phase II) issued its [final reports](http://www.jkcf.or.jp/projects/kaigi/history/). Based the media accounts in Japan and Korea, there seem to have been some progress in narrowing the differences between the two sides, especially regard to ancient and medieval history. Difference over modern history, however, appear to be more difficult to bridge.
