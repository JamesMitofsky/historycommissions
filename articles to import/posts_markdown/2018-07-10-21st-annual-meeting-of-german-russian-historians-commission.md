Together with the annual meeting, a number of public events were held in Munich, July 4-5, 2018. They include a panel discussion on the 1918 Revolution in Moscow, Berlin, and Munich, a day-long symposium at the [Institute for Contemporary History](https://www.ifz-muenchen.de/aktuelles/artikel/datum/2018/07/06/moskau-muenchen-berlin/) and the launch of Volume One of a joint history book of German-Russian relations at the Tolstoi Library.
